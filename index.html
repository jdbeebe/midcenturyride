Three directions

Each one is a working prototype built from all 26 houses on the ride sheet. Open them on your phone too: drag a page edge, swipe, or tap the sides to turn.

ModernNew Canaan
Direction A

The Monograph

A cream-paper architecture book in the spirit of 1950s design monographs. Futura-style type, one persimmon accent, and generous margins.

  • Two-page spreads on desktop, photo left and story right
  • On a phone, each page turns like a real leaf from the left edge
  • An index of the full route up front

Direction B

The Cue Deck

A rider’s cue sheet reimagined as a flip calendar on the handlebars. Swiss type, an Eames palette, and a transit-map route line.

  • Cards flip up from a top hinge, the most tactile option on mobile
  • Stop or Ride-by status, Map links, and a “Next house” cue on every card
  • Full stories in a pull-up sheet

Direction C

After Dark

Cinematic and photography-led. Glass houses glowing at dusk, warm brass on charcoal, and Bodoni headlines. A keepsake rather than a tool.

  • Full-bleed photo per house, landscape on desktop and portrait on phone
  • Grouped by road as you ride
  • “Continue reading” opens the full story on the page
